1. lexical
A word or phrase has multiple distinct meanings.

Example:

Possible meanings:

- a piece of sports equipment

2. syntactic
The grammatical structure allows more than one reasonable interpretation.

Example:
"I saw the man with the telescope."
Possible meanings:
- the speaker used the telescope
- the man had the telescope

3. referential
A pronoun or referring expression could refer to more than one person,
object, or entity.

Example:
"Rahul met Arjun after class. He told him about the project."

"He" and "him" may refer to Rahul or Arjun.

4. pragmatic
The literal sentence is understandable, but the intended meaning or
communicative purpose could reasonably be interpreted in more than one way.
